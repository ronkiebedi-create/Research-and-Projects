## Featured Research & Projects

### **[Multi-Asset Trend-Following Portfolio — Risk Assessment](multi_asset_trend_risk_assessment/project.ipynb)**

Python research project assessing the trade-off between investment growth, volatility and drawdowns across equities, bonds, gold and commodities. Combines three moving-average horizons with inverse-volatility allocation, residual cash interest and transaction costs based on actual drifted holdings.

Evaluated against five benchmarks using annual expanding-history comparisons and **5,000 paired moving-block bootstrap simulations**. In the 2021–2025 evaluation, the enhanced model returned **48.739%** (**8.298% CAGR**), with **6.780% annualised volatility**, **0.726 excess-return Sharpe** and **−8.702% maximum drawdown**. It had the lowest volatility and shallowest maximum drawdown of the six portfolios; fully invested passive portfolios earned higher total returns.

The findings support a **defensive return/risk trade-off**. This is historical research with a pseudo-out-of-sample evaluation, rather than an untouched holdout; bootstrap frequencies are conditional on the observed sample.
